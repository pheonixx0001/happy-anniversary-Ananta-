For You, Ananta

six months · from my heart

My Love,

Six months may look like a small number written on a calendar, but somehow, when I think of you, it feels like a whole little universe of its own. A universe made of your name, your smile, your presence, and all those quiet feelings that are sometimes too beautiful to fit into words. Somewhere between ordinary days and ordinary conversations, you became something incredibly special to me — someone my heart simply feels at home with.

There is a kind of beauty in having one person who can make the smallest things feel meaningful. A message from you can brighten an ordinary moment. Your name appearing on my screen can bring a smile before I even realise I am smiling. And sometimes, just thinking about you is enough to make the world feel a little softer around the edges.

I love you for all the little things that make you you. The warmth in the way you care, the sweetness in the way you love, the little details that make your personality impossible to replace. I love that you are not some perfectly written character from a story. You are real, wonderfully yourself, and that is exactly what makes you so precious to me.

If I could give you one thing and make sure you carried it with you wherever you went, I would give you the feeling of being completely cherished. I would want you to wake up knowing that you matter, to look at yourself and remember that you are beautiful in all the ways that actually count, and to know that your presence has brought a kind of warmth into my life that I never want to take for granted.

I don't think love always needs the loudest words. Sometimes love is simply remembering someone's little details. Sometimes it is wanting to share a random thought with them. Sometimes it is seeing something beautiful and immediately thinking, “I wish she could see this too.” And somehow, you have become that person for me — the person my mind naturally turns towards when something feels beautiful, funny, exciting or simply worth sharing.

You have a place in my heart that feels wonderfully yours. Not because I had to make room for it, but because somehow it appeared naturally, quietly and beautifully. And the more I know you, the more grateful I feel for that little place you hold in my life.

I hope you never underestimate how loved you are. I hope you know that you deserve tenderness, patience, laughter, flowers, pretty skies, peaceful mornings and all the tiny beautiful things that make life feel special. And whenever life gets busy, I hope there is always a small part of you that remembers that somewhere, someone is looking at your existence with an enormous amount of affection.

Six months with you has given the word “us” a meaning that I genuinely treasure. And if there is one thing I want to keep saying, without making it complicated or dramatic, it is simply this: I am so incredibly happy that it is you.

You are one of the softest thoughts in my mind, one of the sweetest names in my heart, and one of the most beautiful parts of my world.

So today, on our six months, I just want to leave you with something simple and completely honest — I love you, Ananta. More softly than words can explain, and more deeply than this little page could ever hold.

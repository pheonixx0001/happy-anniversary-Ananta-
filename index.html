My Love,

I hope you know how much you truly mean to me. Today isn't just another date on the calendar — it's a little reminder of us, of everything we've shared, everything we've learned, and every little moment that brought us closer. Six months with you may sound like a small amount of time, but somehow you've managed to become such a beautiful and important part of my life.

When I look back at these six months, I realise how much you have changed me. You have taught me what it feels like to genuinely care for someone, understand them, be patient with them, and love someone with my whole heart. You have brought a kind of happiness into my life that I never knew I was missing. Without you, I know I would be a very different person today.

And yesterday made me realise all of that again. You didn't just tell me that you love me — you showed me. You proved your love through your actions, and that meant more to me than I could ever properly explain. It reminded me that what we have isn't just a collection of pretty words. It's something we both keep choosing, something we both care about, and something that genuinely matters to both of us.

I want you to know that I choose you too. Not only on the days when everything is perfect, but also on the confusing days, the difficult moments, the misunderstandings and everything in between. I want to keep learning you, understanding you, growing with you and becoming a better person for us.

You are my favourite person to talk to, my favourite name to see appear on my phone, my favourite person to share random thoughts with, and somehow even after all this time, you still manage to make ordinary moments feel special. There is a place in my heart that feels completely yours, and I hope you always know how precious that place is.

I hope you stay beside me like this. I hope we keep laughing at stupid things, annoying each other for absolutely no reason, sharing our little moments, fixing things when they go wrong and celebrating every milestone together. I don't want these six months to simply become a memory — I want them to be the beginning of many more chapters of us.

Please keep being the person who makes ordinary days feel special. And whenever life gets difficult, I hope we remember why we started and choose to understand each other instead of giving up on each other.

I hope you always stay with me like this. I don't want you to suddenly disappear from my life one day and leave me behind. I want us to keep walking together, keep growing together, and keep making beautiful little memories together for as long as life allows us to.

My love for you isn't something I can measure. It's hidden in the smallest things — in the random moments when I think of you, in the way your happiness matters to me, in the little details I remember about you, and in the way even a simple conversation with you can make my entire day better.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for every smile, every conversation, every little effort, every memory, and every time you have made me feel loved. Thank you for being yourself. And thank you for proving your love to me not only through words, but through the things you do.

So today, on our six months, I just want to say this as simply and honestly as I can: I love you, Ananta. I am so incredibly happy that it is you, and if I could choose again, I would still choose you.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like, but I know one thing — I want you in it.

with all my heart, always ♡
